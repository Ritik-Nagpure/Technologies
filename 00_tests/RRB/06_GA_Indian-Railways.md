# INDIAN RAILWAYS – RRB ALP GENERAL AWARENESS

Important current-affairs note: South Coast Railway became operational as a separate zone on 1 June 2026; older study material may still list only 17 zones.

## 1. History of Indian Railways

Q1. When did the first passenger train run in India?
A. 16 April 1853.

Q2. Between which two places did India's first passenger train run?
A. Bori Bunder (Mumbai) and Thane.

Q3. What was the distance covered by India's first passenger train?
A. Approximately 34 kilometres.

Q4. How many coaches did the first passenger train have?
A. 14 coaches.

Q5. How many passengers travelled on India's first passenger train?
A. Approximately 400 passengers.

Q6. Which three locomotives hauled India's first passenger train?
A. Sahib, Sindh and Sultan.

Q7. When did the first passenger train run in eastern India?
A. 15 August 1854, between Howrah and Hooghly.

Q8. When did the first passenger train run in southern India?
A. 1 July 1856, between Royapuram and Wallajah Road.

Q9. When was the Railway Board established?
A. 1905.

Q10. When was the first railway budget presented separately in India?
A. 1924.

Q11. Who presented the first separate Railway Budget?
A. Sir William Acworth is associated with the recommendations that led to its separation; the first separate budget was presented by James H. Thomas in 1924.

Q12. When was the Railway Budget merged with the Union Budget?
A. 2017.

Q13. Who presented the last separate Railway Budget?
A. Suresh Prabhu, in 2016.

Q14. When was Southern Railway formed?
A. 14 April 1951.

Q15. When was Central Railway formed?
A. 5 November 1951.

Q16. When was Western Railway formed?
A. 5 November 1951.

Q17. When was the first electric train introduced in India?
A. 3 February 1925.

Q18. Between which stations did India's first electric train run?
A. Bombay Victoria Terminus and Kurla.

Q19. What was the voltage used by India's first electric train?
A. 1,500 volts DC.

Q20. When was the first metro railway service started in India?
A. 24 October 1984, in Kolkata.

## 2. Indian Railways – Organisation

Q21. Which ministry controls Indian Railways?
A. Ministry of Railways.

Q22. What is the apex administrative body of Indian Railways?
A. Railway Board.

Q23. How many railway zones does Indian Railways have as of November 2026?
A. 18.

A. General Manager (GM).

Q33. What is the full form of IRCTC?

Q35. What is the full form of IRFC?

Q36. What is the main function of IRFC?
A. Raising funds for railway infrastructure and rolling-stock requirements.

## 6. Railway Tracks and Technical Basics

Q96. What are the three major railway gauges historically used in India?
A. Broad gauge, metre gauge and narrow gauge.

Q97. What is the width of Indian broad gauge?
A. 1,676 millimetres.

Q98. What is the width of metre gauge?
A. 1,000 millimetres.

Q99. What is the width of the common narrow gauge?
A. 762 millimetres or 610 millimetres.

Q100. Which gauge is predominantly used on Indian Railways?
A. Broad gauge.

Q101. What is railway track gauge?
A. The distance between the inner faces of the two rails.

Q102. What is the full form of LHB?
A. Linke-Hofmann-Busch.

Q103. What is an LHB coach?
A. A modern passenger coach design used by Indian Railways.

Q104. What is the full form of EMU?
A. Electric Multiple Unit.

Q105. What is the full form of MEMU?
A. Mainline Electric Multiple Unit.

Q106. What is the full form of DEMU?
A. Diesel Electric Multiple Unit.

Q107. What is the function of a railway locomotive?
A. To provide traction for hauling trains.

Q108. What is the function of railway sleepers?
A. To support rails and maintain the correct track gauge.

Q109. What is the purpose of railway ballast?
A. To distribute loads, provide drainage and hold sleepers in position.

Q110. What is a railway signal used for?
A. To control train movements and maintain safe distances between trains.

Q111. What is the function of railway points and crossings?
A. To guide trains from one track to another.

Q112. What is the full form of OHE?
A. Overhead Equipment.

Q113. What is the purpose of OHE?
A. To supply electrical power to electric locomotives through overhead wires.

Q114. What is the standard voltage of India's mainline railway electrification system?
A. 25 kV AC.

Q115. What is the purpose of a pantograph?
A. To collect electrical power from overhead wires.

Q116. What is the function of a railway brake system?
A. To slow down or stop a train safely.

Q117. What is the full form of Kavach in Indian Railways?
A. Kavach is the name of India's indigenous Automatic Train Protection (ATP) system.

Q118. What is the main purpose of Kavach?
A. To help prevent train collisions and overspeeding-related accidents.

Q119. What is the full form of ATP?
A. Automatic Train Protection.

Q120. What is the purpose of a railway level crossing?
A. To allow road traffic to cross railway tracks at the same level.
