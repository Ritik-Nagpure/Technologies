## SECTION 1 – VERIFIED / REPORTED RRB ALP PREVIOUS-YEAR QUESTIONS

**Q1. [RRB ALP 2018]** Which of the following statements about particles of matter is incorrect?

**Answer:** Particles of matter are in a steady state. They are continuously moving.

**Q2. [RRB ALP 2018]** When an iron nail is dipped into copper sulphate solution, which substance gets displaced?

**Answer:** Copper.

**Q3. [RRB ALP 2024]** Which type of chemical reaction involves the exchange of ions between reactants?

**Answer:** Double displacement reaction.

**Q4. [RRB ALP 2024]** What compound is formed when dilute hydrochloric acid is added to copper oxide?

**Answer:** Copper(II) chloride and water.

**Equation:** CuO + 2HCl → CuCl₂ + H₂O

**Q5. [RRB ALP 2024]** Which substance is obtained upon heating ferrous sulphate?

**Answer:** Ferric oxide, sulphur dioxide and sulphur trioxide.

**Equation:** 2FeSO₄ → Fe₂O₃ + SO₂ + SO₃

**Q6. [RRB ALP 2024]** What is the balanced equation for the reaction between iron and steam?

**Answer:** 3Fe + 4H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + 4H₂

**Q7. [RRB ALP 2024]** What type of flame is produced when naphthalene burns?

**Answer:** A sooty flame due to its high carbon content.

**Q8. [RRB ALP 2024]** Which of the following is not an example of a combination reaction?

**Answer:** A decomposition reaction is not a combination reaction. The exact options are required to identify the intended answer.

**Q9. [RRB ALP 2025 – reported question]** What type of reactions are represented by these equations?

I. BaCl₂ + Na₂SO₄ → BaSO₄ + 2NaCl

II. Pb(NO₃)₂ + 2KI → PbI₂ + 2KNO₃

**Answer:** Both are double displacement reactions.

**Q10. [RRB ALP 2024]** Which acid is found in vinegar?

**Answer:** Acetic acid.

## SECTION 3 – WHAT HAPPENS WHEN?

**Q36. [Easy]** What happens when an iron nail is placed in copper sulphate solution?

**Answer:** Iron displaces copper. The blue solution turns green, and a reddish-brown copper coating forms on the nail.

**Q37. [Easy]** What happens when magnesium ribbon burns in air?

**Answer:** It burns with a dazzling white flame and forms white magnesium oxide powder.

**Q38. [Easy]** What happens when dilute hydrochloric acid reacts with zinc?

**Answer:** Hydrogen gas is released with effervescence.

**Q39. [Easy]** What happens when carbon dioxide is passed through limewater?

**Answer:** Limewater turns milky due to the formation of calcium carbonate.

**Q40. [Easy]** What happens when blue copper sulphate crystals are heated?

**Answer:** They lose water of crystallisation and become white.

**Q41. [Moderate]** What happens when copper powder is heated in air?

**Answer:** The reddish-brown copper turns black due to the formation of copper oxide.

**42. [Moderate]** What happens when silver chloride is exposed to sunlight?

**Answer:** It decomposes into silver and chlorine gas. Its white colour turns grey.

2AgCl → 2Ag + Cl₂

**Q43. [Moderate]** What happens when lead nitrate is heated?

**Answer:** It decomposes into lead oxide, nitrogen dioxide and oxygen. Brown fumes of nitrogen dioxide are released.

2Pb(NO₃)₂ → 2PbO + 4NO₂ + O₂

**Q44. [Moderate]** What happens when sodium reacts with water?

**Answer:** Sodium reacts vigorously to form sodium hydroxide and hydrogen gas.

2Na + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + H₂

**Q45. [Moderate]** What happens when chlorine gas reacts with dry slaked lime?

**Answer:** Bleaching powder is formed.

Ca(OH)₂ + Cl₂ → CaOCl₂ + H₂O

**Q46. [Moderate]** What happens when quicklime reacts with water?

**Answer:** A vigorous exothermic reaction occurs, forming slaked lime.

CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂ + Heat

**Q47. [Difficult]** What happens when an aqueous solution of barium chloride is mixed with sodium sulphate?

**Answer:** A white precipitate of barium sulphate forms.

BaCl₂ + Na₂SO₄ → BaSO₄↓ + 2NaCl

**Q48. [Difficult]** What happens when an aqueous solution of lead nitrate is mixed with potassium iodide?

**Answer:** A yellow precipitate of lead iodide forms.

Pb(NO₃)₂ + 2KI → PbI₂↓ + 2KNO₃

**Q49. [Difficult]** What happens when an acid reacts with a metal carbonate?

**Answer:** Salt, water and carbon dioxide are produced.

**Q50. [Difficult]** What happens when an acid reacts with a metal?

**Answer:** Salt and hydrogen gas are generally produced, provided the metal is sufficiently reactive.

## SECTION 4 – IDENTIFY THE REACTION TYPE

**Q51. [Easy]** What type of reaction is represented by A + B → AB?

**Answer:** Combination reaction.

**Q52. [Easy]** What type of reaction is represented by AB → A + B?

**Answer:** Decomposition reaction.

**Q53. [Easy]** What type of reaction is represented by A + BC → AC + B?

**Answer:** Displacement reaction.

**Q54. [Easy]** What type of reaction is represented by AB + CD → AD + CB?

**Answer:** Double displacement reaction.

**Q55. [Easy]** What type of reaction occurs when a substance reacts with oxygen and releases heat and light?

**Answer:** Combustion reaction.

**Q56. [Easy]** What type of reaction occurs when an acid reacts with a base to form salt and water?

**Answer:** Neutralisation reaction.

**Q57. [Moderate]** What type of reaction is represented by 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO?

**Answer:** Combination reaction and oxidation reaction.

**Q58. [Moderate]** What type of reaction is represented by CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂?

**Answer:** Thermal decomposition reaction.

**Q59. [Moderate]** What type of reaction is represented by 2AgCl → 2Ag + Cl₂ in sunlight?

**Answer:** Photochemical decomposition reaction.

**Answer:** Displacement reaction.

**Answer:** Neutralisation reaction and double displacement reaction.

**Q62. [Moderate]** What type of reaction is represented by BaCl₂ + Na₂SO₄ → BaSO₄ + 2NaCl?

**Q63. [Difficult]** What type of reaction occurs when hydrogen reduces copper oxide to copper?

**Answer:** Redox reaction.

CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂O

**Q64. [Difficult]** What type of reaction occurs when iron reacts with oxygen and forms rust?

**Q65. [Difficult]** What type of reaction occurs when a substance gains oxygen?

**Answer:** Oxidation.

**Q66. [Difficult]** What type of reaction occurs when a substance loses oxygen?

## SECTION 6 – IMPORTANT CHEMICAL EQUATIONS TO REMEMBER

**Q81. [Easy]** What is the chemical equation for photosynthesis?

**Answer:** 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (in the presence of sunlight and chlorophyll).

**Q82. [Easy]** What is the chemical equation for respiration using glucose?

**Answer:** C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Energy.

**Q83. [Easy]** What is the chemical equation for rust formation in simplified form?

**Answer:** 4Fe + 3O₂ + 2xH₂O → 2Fe₂O₃·xH₂O.

**Q84. [Moderate]** What is the chemical equation for the chlor-alkali process?

**Answer:** 2NaCl + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + Cl₂ + H₂ (electrolysis of brine).

**Q85. [Moderate]** What is the chemical equation for the formation of baking soda in the Solvay process?

**Answer:** NaCl + NH₃ + CO₂ + H₂O → NaHCO₃ + NH₄Cl.

**Q86. [Moderate]** What is the chemical equation for heating baking soda?

**Answer:** 2NaHCO₃ → Na₂CO₃ + CO₂ + H₂O.

**Q87. [Moderate]** What is the chemical equation for the formation of washing soda?

**Answer:** Na₂CO₃ + 10H₂O → Na₂CO₃·10H₂O.

**Q88. [Moderate]** What is the chemical equation for the formation of plaster of Paris from gypsum?

**Answer:** CaSO₄·2H₂O → CaSO₄·½H₂O + 1½H₂O (on heating).

**Q89. [Moderate]** What is the chemical equation for setting of plaster of Paris?

**Answer:** CaSO₄·½H₂O + 1½H₂O → CaSO₄·2H₂O.

**Q90. [Difficult]** What is the chemical equation for the reaction between ethanol and ethanoic acid?

**Answer:** CH₃COOH + C₂H₅OH → CH₃COOC₂H₅ + H₂O (in the presence of concentrated H₂SO₄).

**Q91. [Difficult]** What is the chemical equation for saponification of ethyl ethanoate with sodium hydroxide?

**Answer:** CH₃COOC₂H₅ + NaOH → CH₃COONa + C₂H₅OH.

**Q92. [Difficult]** What is the chemical equation for the hydrogenation of ethene?

**Answer:** C₂H₄ + H₂ → C₂H₆ (in the presence of nickel catalyst).

## 1. Combination Reactions

**1. Formation of water**
- Reactants: Hydrogen + Oxygen
- Product: Water
- Equation: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O

**2. Formation of ammonia (Haber process)**
- Reactants: Nitrogen + Hydrogen
- Product: Ammonia
- Equation: N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃
- Catalyst: Iron

**3. Formation of magnesium oxide**
- Reactants: Magnesium + Oxygen
- Product: Magnesium oxide
- Equation: 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO

**4. Formation of calcium oxide**
- Reactants: Calcium + Oxygen
- Product: Calcium oxide
- Equation: 2Ca + O₂ → 2CaO

**5. Formation of hydrogen chloride**
- Reactants: Hydrogen + Chlorine
- Product: Hydrogen chloride
- Equation: H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl

## 2. Decomposition Reactions

**6. Thermal decomposition of calcium carbonate (Limestone)**
- Reactant: Calcium carbonate
- Products: Calcium oxide + Carbon dioxide
- Equation: CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂
- Process: Calcination

**7. Thermal decomposition of ferrous sulphate**
- Reactant: Ferrous sulphate
- Products: Ferric oxide + Sulphur dioxide + Sulphur trioxide
- Equation: 2FeSO₄ → Fe₂O₃ + SO₂ + SO₃

**8. Thermal decomposition of lead nitrate**
- Reactant: Lead nitrate
- Products: Lead oxide + Nitrogen dioxide + Oxygen
- Equation: 2Pb(NO₃)₂ → 2PbO + 4NO₂ + O₂

**9. Thermal decomposition of potassium chlorate**
- Reactant: Potassium chlorate
- Products: Potassium chloride + Oxygen
- Equation: 2KClO₃ → 2KCl + 3O₂
- Catalyst: Manganese dioxide

**10. Photochemical decomposition of silver chloride**
- Reactant: Silver chloride
- Products: Silver + Chlorine
- Equation: 2AgCl → 2Ag + Cl₂
- Condition: Sunlight

**11. Photochemical decomposition of silver bromide**
- Reactant: Silver bromide
- Products: Silver + Bromine
- Equation: 2AgBr → 2Ag + Br₂
- Condition: Sunlight

**12. Electrolytic decomposition of water**
- Reactant: Water
- Products: Hydrogen + Oxygen
- Equation: 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂
- Condition: Electricity

## 3. Displacement Reactions

**13. Thermite reaction**
- Reactants: Aluminium + Ferric oxide

- Equation: Fe₂O₃ + 2Al → Al₂O₃ + 2Fe

**17. Zinc and hydrochloric acid**

- Equation: Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂

**18. Iron and dilute sulphuric acid**

- Equation: Fe + H₂SO₄ → FeSO₄ + H₂

## 7. Important Industrial and Everyday Reactions

- Reactants: Nitrogen + Hydrogen

- Equation: N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃
- Catalyst: Iron

- Reactants: Sulphur trioxide + Water
- Product: Sulphuric acid
- Equation: SO₃ + H₂O → H₂SO₄

**34. Chlor-Alkali Process**
- Reactants: Sodium chloride solution + Water
- Products: Sodium hydroxide + Chlorine + Hydrogen
- Equation: 2NaCl + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + Cl₂ + H₂
- Condition: Electrolysis of brine

**35. Solvay Process – Formation of baking soda**
- Reactants: Sodium chloride + Ammonia + Carbon dioxide + Water
- Products: Sodium bicarbonate + Ammonium chloride
- Equation: NaCl + NH₃ + CO₂ + H₂O → NaHCO₃ + NH₄Cl

- Product: Bleaching powder
- Equation: Ca(OH)₂ + Cl₂ → CaOCl₂ + H₂O

**38. Formation of plaster of Paris**

- Equation: CaSO₄·2H₂O → CaSO₄·½H₂O + 1½H₂O

**39. Setting of plaster of Paris**

- Product: Gypsum
- Equation: CaSO₄·½H₂O + 1½H₂O → CaSO₄·2H₂O

**40. Formation of slaked lime**
