## Important Notes

1. India has 28 states and 8 Union Territories.
2. Lok Sabha MPs and Rajya Sabha MPs are listed separately.
3. MLAs represent elected Legislative Assembly seats.
4. Delhi, Jammu and Kashmir, and Puducherry have Legislative Assemblies among the Union Territories.

6. District counts are subject to administrative changes.
7. `—` means no second capital or no comparable figure supplied.
8. Administrative division counts are not included because definitions and reporting dates vary between states.
