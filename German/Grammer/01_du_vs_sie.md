# 👥 German Grammar 1 — du vs Sie (Formal vs Informal)

> **Goal:** Never use the wrong "you" — the #1 social grammar rule in German.
> 📝 All examples German. Loanwords marked ⚠️.

---

## 1. The Two Systems

| System | Pronoun | Verb Form | Used With |
|---|---|---|---|
| Informal | du (singular) | 2nd person singular | friends, family, children |
| Informal | ihr (plural) | 2nd person plural | group of friends |
| Formal | Sie (always capital) | 3rd person plural | strangers, officials, elders |

Source: 

---

## 2. When to Use **du**

| Situation | Example | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Family | Kommst **du** morgen? | Family always du |
| Friends | Wie geht's **dir**? | Peers = du |
| Children | Hast **du** Hunger? | All children get du |
| Sports/leisure | Spielst **du** Fußball? | Informal settings = du |
| Young people (11th grade+) | **Du** bist neu hier? | Students often du each other |

Source: 

---

## 3. When to Use **Sie**

| Situation | Example | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Strangers (adults) | Wie heißen **Sie**? | Not yet acquainted |
| Workplace (new colleague) | Können **Sie** das erklären? | Wait for du offer |
| Shops/hotels | Haben **Sie** das? | Public life = Sie |
| Police/officials | Zeigen **Sie** Ihren Ausweis. | Always Sie |
| Elderly people | Wie geht es **Ihnen**? | Respect |

Source: 

---

## 4. Verb Conjugation: du vs Sie

| Verb | du (informal) | Sie (formal) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| kommen | **du kommst** | **Sie kommen** | come |
| wohnen | **du wohnst** | **Sie wohnen** | live |
| sein | **du bist** | **Sie sind** | be |
| haben | **du hast** | **Sie haben** | have |
| arbeiten | **du arbeitest** | **Sie arbeiten** | work |

⚠️ **Trap:** *Sie kommen* looks like infinitive, but it is the **3rd person plural** form. *Sie sein* is wrong — always *Sie sind*.

## 5. The Formal Question Pattern

| Informal | Formal | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Wie heißt **du**? | Wie heißen **Sie**? | What's your name? |
| Woher kommst **du**? | Woher kommen **Sie**? | Where are you from? |
| Wo wohnst **du**? | Wo wohnen **Sie**? | Where do you live? |
| Was machst **du**? | Was machen **Sie**? | What do you do? |

Source: 

---

## 6. The Informal Question Pattern (du)

| Question | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Wie heißt du? | *vee hyst doo* | What's your name? |
| Woher kommst du? | *voh-HAIR komst doo* | Where are you from? |
| Wo wohnst du? | *voh vohnst doo* | Where do you live? |
| Wie geht's dir? | *vee gayts deer* | How are you? |

Source: 

---

## 7. The Formal Question Pattern (Sie)

| Question | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Wie heißen Sie? | *vee HY-sen zee* | What's your name? |
| Woher kommen Sie? | *voh-HAIR KOM-men zee* | Where are you from? |
| Wo wohnen Sie? | *voh VOH-nen zee* | Where do you live? |
| Wie geht es Ihnen? | *vee gayt es EE-nen* | How are you? |

Source:

## 8. ⚠️ Switching from Sie to du ("das Du anbieten")

| Phrase | Sounds Like | Meaning | Register |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wollen wir uns duzen? | *VOL-len veer oons DOO-tsen* | Shall we use du? | Offer |
| Wir können uns gern duzen. | *veer KUR-nen oons gairn DOO-tsen* | We can use du. | Offer |
| Du kannst gern du zu mir sagen. | *doo kannst gairn doo tsoo meer ZAH-gen* | Feel free to say du. | Offer |
| Gerne! Ich bin Anna. | *GAIR-neh! ikh bin AH-nah* | Gladly! I'm Anna. | Accept |

🔑 **Rule:** Never switch to *du* uninvited in a formal setting. Wait for the offer.

## 9. du vs ihr (Informal Plural)

| Pronoun | Use | Verb Form | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| du | one person | 2nd singular | Kommst **du** mit? |
| ihr | multiple people | 2nd plural | Kommt **ihr** mit? |

## 10. ✅ Daily Drill

1. Ask 3 questions in **du** form, then rewrite them in **Sie** form.
2. Practice: *Wie geht's dir?* vs *Wie geht es Ihnen?*
3. Memorize: **du = friends/family** · **Sie = strangers/officials**.
