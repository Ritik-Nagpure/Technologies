# 🏗️ German Grammar 2 — Verb Position (Position 2 Rule)

> **Goal:** Build correct German sentences — verb is ALWAYS in position 2.
> 📝 All examples German.

---

## 1. The Golden Rule

In a German **statement**, the conjugated verb is **always the second element**.

| Position 1 | Position 2 (Verb) | Position 3+ | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ich | **trinke** | Wasser. | I drink water. |
| Wir | **lernen** | Deutsch. | We learn German. |
| Er | **ist** | Lehrer. | He is a teacher. |

---

## 2. What Can Be Position 1?

| Element | Example | Verb stays position 2? |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | **Ich** trinke Wasser. | ✅ |
| Time word | **Heute** trinke ich Wasser. | ✅ (subject moves after verb) |
| Place word | **In Berlin** wohne ich. | ✅ |
| Adverb | **Gern** trinke ich Kaffee. | ✅ |

Source: 

---

## 3. ⚠️ Inversion: Subject Moves After Verb

If position 1 is **not** the subject, the subject goes **after the verb** (position 3).

| Wrong | Correct | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Heute ich gehe ins Kino. | **Heute gehe ich** ins Kino. | Today I go to the cinema. |
| In Berlin ich wohne. | **In Berlin wohne ich**. | In Berlin I live. |
| Gern ich trinke Kaffee. | **Gern trinke ich** Kaffee. | I like drinking coffee. |

🔑 **Rule:** Whatever is in position 1, verb is position 2. Subject follows if not in position 1.

---

## 4. Yes/No Questions — Verb Position 1

For questions expecting "yes" or "no," the verb moves to **position 1**.

| Position 1 (Verb) | Position 2 (Subject) | Position 3+ | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trinkst** | du | Wasser? | Do you drink water? |
| **Bist** | du | müde? | Are you tired? |
| **Haben** | Sie | Zeit? | Do you have time? |
| **Kommen** | Sie | aus Deutschland? | Are you from Germany? |

Source: 

---

## 5. W-Questions — Verb Position 2

For information questions: **W-word (position 1) + Verb (position 2) + Subject (position 3)**.

| Position 1 (W-word) | Position 2 (Verb) | Position 3 (Subject) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wie** | **heißt** | du? | What's your name? |
| **Wo** | **wohnst** | du? | Where do you live? |
| **Was** | **machst** | du? | What do you do? |
| **Wann** | **kommst** | du? | When are you coming? |

Source: 

---

## 6. Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Wrong | Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verb in position 3 | Ich Wasser trinke. | **Ich trinke Wasser.** | Verb must be position 2 |
| No inversion | Heute ich gehe. | **Heute gehe ich.** | Subject follows verb |
| W-question wrong order | Wo du wohnst? | **Wo wohnst du?** | Verb before subject |
| No verb in yes/no Q | Du bist müde? | **Bist du müde?** | Verb goes to position 1 |
