# 📦 German Grammar 3 — Nominative vs Accusative

> **Goal:** Know when to use der/die/das vs den/die/das.
> 📝 All examples German.

---

## 1. What Are Cases?

German changes articles based on the noun's **role** in the sentence.

| Case | Function | Question |
|---|---|---|
| **Nominative** | Subject (who does the action) | Wer? (who?) |
| **Accusative** | Direct object (what receives action) | Wen? (whom?) |

---

## 2. Nominative Articles (Subject)

| Gender | Definite | Indefinite | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Masculine | **der** | **ein** | **Der** Mann trinkt. |
| Feminine | **die** | **eine** | **Die** Frau trinkt. |
| Neuter | **das** | **ein** | **Das** Kind trinkt. |
| Plural | **die** | — (keine) | **Die** Kinder trinken. |

Source: 

---

## 3. Accusative Articles (Direct Object)

⚠️ **Only masculine changes!**

| Gender | Definite | Indefinite | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Masculine | **den** (der→den) | **einen** (ein→einen) | Ich trinke **einen** Kaffee. |
| Feminine | **die** (no change) | **eine** (no change) | Ich trinke **eine** Limonade. |
| Neuter | **das** (no change) | **ein** (no change) | Ich trinke **ein** Bier. |
| Plural | **die** (no change) | — (keine) | Ich trinke **keine** Cola. |

Source: 

---

## 4. Verbs That Take Accusative

| Verb | Meaning | Example (Accusative) |
|---|---|---|
| trinken | to drink | Ich trinke **einen** Tee. |
| essen | to eat | Er isst **einen** Apfel. |
| haben | to have | Sie hat **eine** Frage. |
| bestellen | to order | Wir bestellen **einen** Kaffee. |
| nehmen | to take | Nimmst du **das** Buch? |
| möchten | would like | Ich möchte **einen** Saft. |

## 5. Nominative vs Accusative — Side by Side

| Sentence | Case | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Der** Mann trinkt **den** Kaffee. | Nom + Akk | der = subject, den = object |
| **Die** Frau isst **den** Apfel. | Nom + Akk | die = subject, den = object |
| **Das** Kind hat **eine** Frage. | Nom + Akk | das = subject, eine = object |

Source: 

---

## 6. The #1 Trap: der → den

| Wrong | Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Ich trinke **der** Kaffee. | Ich trinke **den** Kaffee. | Masculine object = den |
| Ich möchte **ein** Kaffee. | Ich möchte **einen** Kaffee. | Masculine object = einen |

🔑 **Drill:** *der* becomes *den* when it is the direct object. *ein* becomes *einen*.
