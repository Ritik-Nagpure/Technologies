# 🏋️ German Pronunciation 4 — Drills, Shadowing & Checklist

> **Goal:** Turn rules into habits — daily drills, shadowing, and self-checks.
> 📝 All example words are **German**. Words marked **⚠️** are loanwords.

---

## 1. The 10-Minute Daily Routine

Follow this structure **every day** during Week 1.

| Time | Activity | What To Do | Example |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| 2 min | Alphabet aloud | Say all 26 + umlauts | A–Z, ä, ö, ü, ß |
| 3 min | Word lists | Read pron_1/2/3 lists | Wasser · Vater · Zeit |
| 3 min | Shadowing | Repeat after native audio | Guten Tag! Wie geht's? |
| 2 min | Record & compare | Note your mistakes | Playback vs. native clip |

---

## 2. Minimal Pairs — Vowels

Practice pairs that differ **only in vowel length**.

| Pair | Sounds Like | Meaning | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stadt / Staat | *shtaht* / *shtaht* | city / state | short vs long **a** |
| ihn / in | *een* / *in* | him / in | long vs short **i** |
| Beet / Bett | *bayt* / *bet* | flowerbed / bed | long vs short **e** |

---

## 3. Minimal Pairs — Consonants

Practice pairs that differ **only in one consonant**.

| Pair | Sounds Like | Meaning | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| ich / isch | *ikh* / *ish* | I / suffix | soft *ch* vs *sch* |
| Bach / Buch | *bahkh* / *bookh* | stream / book | *a* vs *u* before *ch* |
| Wein / Bein | *vine* / *bine* | wine / leg | *W* vs *B* |

## 4. Shadowing Script 1 — Greetings

Listen to a native clip, then **repeat immediately**.

| Line | German | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hallo! Guten Tag! | *HAH-loh! GOO-ten tahk!* | Hello! Good day! |
| 2 | Wie geht's? | *vee GAYTS?* | How are you? |
| 3 | Mir geht es gut, danke. | *meer gayt es goot, DAHN-keh* | I'm fine, thanks. |
| 4 | Und dir? | *unt deer?* | And you? |
| 5 | Tschüss! Auf Wiedersehen! | *chooss! ouf VEE-der-zay-en!* | Bye! Goodbye! |

## 5. Shadowing Script 2 — Numbers

Read aloud, then shadow a native speaker.

| Line | German | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | eins, zwei, drei, vier, fünf | *ints, tsvy, dry, feer, fuenf* | 1–5 |
| 2 | sechs, sieben, acht, neun, zehn | *zeks, ZEE-ben, ahkht, noyn, tsayn* | 6–10 |
| 3 | elf, zwölf, dreizehn, vierzehn | *elf, tsvoelf, TSVY-tsayn, FEER-tsayn* | 11–14 |

| 5 | hundert, tausend | *HOON-dert, TOW-zent* | 100, 1000 |

## 6. Shadowing Script 3 — Common Phrases

Practice **rhythm and intonation**.

| Line | German | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ich heiße … | *ikh HY-seh* | My name is … |
| 2 | Ich komme aus … | *ikh KOM-meh ouss* | I come from … |
| 3 | Ich wohne in … | *ikh VOH-neh in* | I live in … |
| 4 | Ich lerne Deutsch. | *ikh LAIR-neh doytsh* | I'm learning German. |
| 5 | Sprechen Sie Englisch? | *SHPREKH-en zee ENG-lish?* | Do you speak English? |

## 7. Shadowing Script 4 — Tricky Sounds

Focus on **ch, r, and umlauts**.

| Line | German | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | ich, mich, Mädchen | *ikh, mikh, MED-chen* | I, me, girl |
| 2 | Bach, Buch, Nacht | *bahkh, bookh, nahkht* | stream, book, night |
| 3 | rot, Brot, Straße | *roht, broht, SHTRAH-seh* | red, bread, street |
| 4 | schön, müde, fünf | *shurn, MUE-deh, fuenf* | beautiful, tired, five |

## 8. Self-Recording Checklist (Part 1)

Record yourself **weekly** and check these points.

| # | Check | What To Listen For | Status |
|:---:|---|---|:---:|
| 1 | Long vs short vowels | Is *Stadt* shorter than *Staat*? | ☐ |
| 2 | W = *v* | Is *Wasser* said *VAS-ser*? | ☐ |
| 3 | V = *f* | Is *Vater* said *FAH-ter*? | ☐ |
| 4 | Z = *ts* | Is *Zeit* said *tsite*? | ☐ |
| 5 | R uvular | Is *rot* from the throat? | ☐ |

More checks for the recording.

## 10. Common Mistakes Log

Write **one line** for every repeated error.

| Mistake | Correction | Example Word | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Said *w* as English W | Say *v* | Wasser | *VAS-ser* |
| Said *z* as English Z | Say *ts* | Zeit | *tsite* |
| Rolled R | Use uvular R | rot | *roht* |
| Short long vowels | Hold them longer | Tag | *tahk* |
| Ignored final devoicing | Devoice end consonants | und | *unt* |

## 16. ✅ Final Reminder

**Consistency beats intensity.**

| Principle | What It Means | Example | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily > weekly | 10 min/day > 2 hrs/week | 10 min shadowing | Builds muscle memory |
| Speak aloud | Even alone | Read word lists aloud | Trains mouth + ear |
| Record weekly | Measure progress | Compare to native | Shows real improvement |
