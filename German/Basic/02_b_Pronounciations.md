# 🔉 German Pronunciation 2 — Consonants

> **Goal:** Master the consonant rules that differ most from English.
> 📝 All example words are **German**. Words marked **⚠️** are loanwords.

---

## 1. W = *v*

German **W** sounds like English **V**.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| W = *v* | Wasser | *VAS-ser* | water |
| W = *v* | Wein | *vine* | wine |
| W = *v* | wohnen | *VOH-nen* | to live |

⚠️ **Never** say *w* as in English "water".

---

## 2. V = *f*

German **V** sounds like English **F** (in most native words).

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| V = *f* | Vater | *FAH-ter* | father |
| V = *f* | viel | *feel* | much |
| V = *f* | von | *fon* | from |

💡 **Exception:** V in loanwords may sound like *v* (e.g., Vase ⚠️ → *VAH-zeh*).

---

## 3. Z = *ts*

German **Z** is **always** *ts*.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Z = *ts* | Zeit | *tsite* | time |
| Z = *ts* | Zug | *tsook* | train |
| Z = *ts* | zehn | *tsayn* | ten |

---

## 4. S at Word Start = *z*

**S** before a vowel at word start sounds like English **Z**.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| S (start) = *z* | Sonne | *ZON-neh* | sun |
| S (start) = *z* | sagen | *ZAH-gen* | to say |
| S (start) = *z* | sieben | *ZEE-ben* | seven |

---

## 5. S Elsewhere = *s*

**S** in the middle or end sounds like English **S**.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| S (mid/end) = *s* | Haus | *house* | house |
| S (mid/end) = *s* | ist | *ist* | is |
| S (mid/end) = *s* | Wespe | *VES-peh* | wasp |

---

## 6. Sch = *sh*

**sch** is always *sh*.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| sch = *sh* | Schule | *SHOO-leh* | school |
| sch = *sh* | Fisch | *fish* | fish |
| sch = *sh* | schön | *shurn* | beautiful |

---

## 7. Sp at Word Start = *shp*

**sp** at the start of a word = *shp*.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| sp (start) = *shp* | Sport | *shport* | sport |
| sp (start) = *shp* | spät | *shpayt* | late |
| sp (start) = *shp* | Sprache | *SHPRAH-kheh* | language |

---

## 8. St at Word Start = *sht*

**st** at the start of a word = *sht*.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| st (start) = *sht* | Stadt | *shtaht* | city |
| st (start) = *sht* | Straße | *SHTRAH-seh* | street |
| st (start) = *sht* | Student ⚠️ | *shtoo-DENT* | student |

---

## 9. Ch — Soft *ich* Sound

After **i / e / ä / ö / ü**, **ch** = soft *kh* (like a hiss).

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| ch (after i/e) = *kh* | ich | *ikh* | I |
| ch (after i/e) = *kh* | mich | *mikh* | me |
| ch (after i/e) = *kh* | Mädchen | *MED-khen* | girl |

---

## 10. Ch — Guttural *ach* Sound

After **a / o / u**, **ch** = guttural *kh* (from the back of the throat).

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| ch (after a/o/u) = *kh* | Bach | *bahkh* | stream |
| ch (after a/o/u) = *kh* | Buch | *bookh* | book |
| ch (after a/o/u) = *kh* | Nacht | *nahkht* | night |

---

## 11. R — Uvular

German **R** is produced at the **back of the throat** (uvular), not rolled.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| R = throat sound | rot | *roht* | red |
| R = throat sound | Brot | *broht* | bread |
| R = throat sound | Straße | *SHTRAH-seh* | street |

💡 **R after a vowel** often softens to *ah* (e.g., Vater → *FAH-tah*).

---

## 12. H — Pronounced vs Silent

**H at word start** is pronounced; **after a vowel** it lengthens the vowel (silent).

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| H (start) = pronounced | Haus | *house* | house |
| H (after vowel) = silent | sehen | *ZAY-en* | to see |
| H (after vowel) = silent | Uhr | *oor* | clock |

---

## 13. J = *y*

German **J** sounds like English **Y**.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| J = *y* | Jahr | *yahr* | year |
| J = *y* | jung | *yoong* | young |
| J = *y* | ja | *yah* | yes |

---

## 14. Q = *kv*

German **Q** (always followed by **u**) = *kv*.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q = *kv* | Quelle | *KVEL-leh* | source |
| Q = *kv* | Qualität ⚠️ | *kvah-li-TAYT* | quality |
| Q = *kv* | quer | *kvehr* | across |

---

## 15. Consonant Clusters

German allows heavy clusters — **pronounce every consonant**.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| cluster = all heard | Strumpf | *shtroompf* | stocking |
| cluster = all heard | Angst | *ahngst* | fear |
| cluster = all heard | Herbst | *hairpst* | autumn |

---

## 16. Final Devoicing

Voiced consonants at word end become **voiceless** (b→p, d→t, g→k).

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| end d → *t* | und | *unt* | and |
| end g → *k* | Tag | *tahk* | day |
| end b → *p* | gab | *gahp* | gave |

---

## 17. Practice List

Read aloud daily, **focusing on the trickiest consonants**:

> Wasser · Vater · Zeit · Sonne · Schule · Sport · Stadt
> ich · Bach · Buch · Nacht · rot · Brot · Jahr · Quelle
