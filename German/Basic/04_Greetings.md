# 👋 German Greetings & Introductions

> **Goal:** Greet anyone correctly (formal vs informal) and introduce yourself in 4 sentences.
> 📝 All example words are German. Loanwords marked ⚠️.

---

## 1. Greetings by Time of Day

| German | Sounds Like | Meaning | When to Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guten Morgen | *GOO-ten MOR-gen* | Good morning | Until ~11 AM |
| Guten Tag | *GOO-ten tahk* | Good day | 11 AM – 6 PM (formal) |
| Guten Abend | *GOO-ten AH-bent* | Good evening | After 6 PM |
| Gute Nacht | *GOO-teh nahkht* | Good night | Only when going to bed |

## 3. ⚠️ du vs Sie — The Critical Rule

German has two "you" forms. Using the wrong one is a social error.

| Form | Pronoun | Use With | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Informal | du | Friends, family, children, peers | Wie heißt **du**? |
| Formal | Sie (always capital) | Strangers, elders, professionals | Wie heißen **Sie**? |

🔑 **Rule:** When in doubt, use **Sie**. Switching to **du** requires the other person's permission.

## 13. ✅ Daily Drill

1. Say **all greetings** aloud (formal + informal).
2. Practice the **du/Sie switch** — ask 3 questions in both forms.
3. Introduce yourself in **4 sentences**: name → origin → city → age.
