# 🎯 German Pronunciation 3 — Endings, Stress & Combinations

> **Goal:** Sound natural by mastering word endings, stress, and suffixes.
> 📝 All example words are **German**. Words marked **⚠️** are loanwords.

---

## 1. -er at Word End = *ah*

Final **-er** softens to a short *ah* sound.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| -er = *ah* | Vater | *FAH-tah* | father |
| -er = *ah* | Mutter | *MOO-tah* | mother |
| -er = *ah* | Lehrer | *LAY-rah* | teacher |

---

## 2. -en at Word End = *n*

Final **-en** often drops the *e* and sounds like *n*.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| -en = *n* | machen | *MAKHN* | to do |
| -en = *n* | haben | *HAHBN* | to have |
| -en = *n* | sprechen | *SHPREKHN* | to speak |

---

## 3. -ig at Word End = *ich*

Final **-ig** sounds like *ich* (soft *kh*).

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| -ig = *ikh* | wichtig | *VIKH-tikh* | important |
| -ig = *ikh* | billig | *BIL-likh* | cheap |
| -ig = *ikh* | ruhig | *ROO-ikh* | quiet |

---

## 4. -tion = *tsee-ohn*

Words ending in **-tion** sound like *tsee-ohn*.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| -tion = *tsee-ohn* | Information ⚠️ | *in-for-mah-TSEE-OHN* | information |
| -tion = *tsee-ohn* | Nation ⚠️ | *nah-TSEE-OHN* | nation |
| -tion = *tsee-ohn* | Situation ⚠️ | *zee-too-ah-TSEE-OHN* | situation |

---

## 5. Inseparable Prefixes (Unstressed)

**be-, ge-, ver-, ent-, er-, zer-** carry **no stress**.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| stress on stem | besuchen | *beh-ZOO-khen* | to visit |
| stress on stem | verstehen | *fair-SHTAY-en* | to understand |
| stress on stem | erklären | *air-KLAY-ren* | to explain |

---

## 6. Separable Prefixes (Stressed)

**an-, auf-, aus-, ein-, mit-, vor-, zu-** carry the **stress**.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| stress on prefix | anfangen | *AN-fahng-en* | to begin |
| stress on prefix | aufstehen | *AUF-shtay-en* | to get up |
| stress on prefix | einkaufen | *INE-kow-fen* | to shop |

---

## 7. Compound Word Stress

Stress falls on the **FIRST element** of a compound.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| stress on 1st part | Haustür | *HOUS-tuer* | front door |
| stress on 1st part | Kindergarten | *KIN-der-gar-ten* | kindergarten |
| stress on 1st part | Bahnhof | *BAHN-hohf* | train station |

---

## 8. Final Devoicing (Reminder)

Voiced consonants at word end become **voiceless**.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| end d → *t* | und | *unt* | and |
| end g → *k* | Tag | *tahk* | day |
| end b → *p* | lieb | *leep* | dear |

---

## 9. -chen and -lein (Diminutives)

These suffixes are **always neuter** and stress the **preceding syllable**.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| -chen = *khen* | Mädchen | *MED-khen* | girl |
| -chen = *khen* | Brötchen | *BRURT-khen* | bread roll |
| -lein = *line* | Fräulein | *FROY-line* | miss (old-fashioned) |

---

## 10. -ung Endings

**-ung** is always **feminine** and pronounced *oong*.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| -ung = *oong* | Zeitung | *TSY-toong* | newspaper |
| -ung = *oong* | Wohnung | *VOH-noong* | apartment |
| -ung = *oong* | Übung | *UE-boong* | exercise |

---

## 11. -heit and -keit Endings

Both are **feminine** and stressed on the **first syllable**.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| -heit = *hite* | Freiheit | *FRY-hite* | freedom |
| -keit = *kite* | Möglichkeit | *MURKH-likh-kite* | possibility |
| -heit = *hite* | Sicherheit | *ZIKH-er-hite* | security |

---

## 12. -bar Endings

**-bar** means "-able" and is stressed on the **first syllable**.

| Rule | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| -bar = *bar* | machbar | *MAKH-bar* | doable |
| -bar = *bar* | essbar | *ESS-bar* | edible |
| -bar = *bar* | sichtbar | *ZIKHT-bar* | visible |

---

## 13. Common Phrase Stress

Stress the **content word** in short phrases.

| Rule | Phrase (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| stress content word | Guten Morgen | *GOO-ten MOR-gen* | good morning |
| stress content word | Auf Wiedersehen | *ouf VEE-der-zay-en* | goodbye |
| stress content word | Wie geht's? | *vee GAYTS* | how are you? |

---

## 14. Sentence-Level Stress

In a statement, stress the **most important word**.

| Rule | Sentence (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| stress the object | Ich trinke Wasser. | *ikh TRIN-keh VAS-ser* | I drink water. |
| stress the time | Ich gehe heute ins Kino. | *ikh GAY-he HOY-teh ins KEE-noh* | I'm going to the cinema today. |
| stress the negation | Ich kann heute nicht kommen. | *ikh kahn HOY-teh nikht KOM-men* | I can't come today. |
