# 🏗️ German Sentence Structure

> **Goal:** Build correct statements, yes/no questions, and W-questions.
> 📝 All example words are German.

---

## 1. ⚠️ The Golden Rule: Verb in Position 2

In a German statement, the **conjugated verb is ALWAYS the second element**.

| Position 1 | Position 2 (Verb) | Position 3+ | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ich | trinke | Wasser. | I drink water. |
| Lee | studiert | Chemie. | Lee studies chemistry. |
| Wir | lernen | Deutsch. | We learn German. |

Source: [citation:4][citation:13]

---

## 2. Inverted Word Order (Time/Place First)

If you start with something other than the subject, the **subject moves after the verb** — but the verb stays in position 2.

| Position 1 | Position 2 (Verb) | Position 3 (Subject) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heute | gehe | ich | ins Kino. |
| Im Sommer | studiere | ich | nicht. |
| Jeden Abend | arbeite | ich | von 17 bis 22 Uhr. |

🔑 **Rule:** Whatever is in position 1, the verb is in position 2. Subject follows the verb if it's not in position 1.

Source: [citation:4][citation:13]

---

## 3. Yes/No Questions — Verb Moves to Position 1

For questions expecting "yes" or "no," put the **verb first**.

| Position 1 (Verb) | Position 2 (Subject) | Position 3+ | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trinkst | du | Wasser? | Do you drink water? |
| Bist | du | müde? | Are you tired? |
| Kommen | Sie | aus Deutschland? | Do you come from Germany? |
| Haben | Sie | Zeit? | Do you have time? |

Source: [citation:4][citation:13]

---

## 4. W-Questions — Question Word + Verb + Subject

For information questions, the **question word is position 1, verb position 2, subject position 3**.

| Position 1 (W-word) | Position 2 (Verb) | Position 3 (Subject) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wie | heißt | du? | What's your name? |
| Wo | wohnst | du? | Where do you live? |
| Woher | kommst | du? | Where do you come from? |
| Was | machst | du? | What are you doing? |
| Wann | kommst | du? | When are you coming? |

Source: [citation:4][citation:13]

---

## 5. Essential Question Words

| German | Sounds Like | Meaning | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| was | *vahs* | what | Was machst du? |
| wer | *vair* | who | Wer ist das? |
| wo | *voh* | where (location) | Wo wohnst du? |
| woher | *voh-HAIR* | where from | Woher kommst du? |
| wohin | *voh-HIN* | where to | Wohin gehst du? |
| wann | *vahn* | when | Wann kommst du? |
| wie | *vee* | how | Wie heißt du? |
| warum | *vah-ROOM* | why | Warum lernst du Deutsch? |

Source: [citation:4][citation:13]

## 6. Statement vs Question — Side by Side

| Type | German | English | Verb Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Statement | Du trinkst Wasser. | You drink water. | Position 2 |
| Yes/No Q | Trinkst du Wasser? | Do you drink water? | Position 1 |
| W-Question | Was trinkst du? | What do you drink? | W-word + Verb (pos. 2) |

Source: [citation:4][citation:13]

---

## 7. Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Wrong | Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verb in position 3 | Ich Wasser trinke. | Ich trinke Wasser. | Verb must be position 2 |
| Forgetting inversion | Heute ich gehe. | Heute gehe ich. | Subject follows verb |
| W-question wrong order | Wo du wohnst? | Wo wohnst du? | Verb before subject |
| No verb in yes/no Q | Du bist müde? | Bist du müde? | Verb goes to position 1 |

Source: [citation:4][citation:13]

## 8. Practice — Build the Sentence

| Prompt | Correct German |
|---|---|
| (I / drink / water) | Ich trinke Wasser. |
| (Today / go / I / to the cinema) | Heute gehe ich ins Kino. |
| (You / are / tired?) — yes/no | Bist du müde? |
| (Where / live / you?) | Wo wohnst du? |
| (What / do / you?) | Was machst du? |

## 9. ✅ Daily Drill

1. Write **3 statements** with verb in position 2.
2. Rewrite them starting with a time word (invert subject/verb).
3. Turn each into a **yes/no question**.
4. Turn each into a **W-question**.
