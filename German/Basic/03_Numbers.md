## 4. ⚠️ THE TRAP: 21–99 — Unit FIRST

**Formula:** unit + **und** + ten. "One-and-twenty," not "twenty-one."

| Number | German | Sounds Like | Formula |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| 21 | einundzwanzig | *INE-oont-tsvahn-tsikh* | **ein** (not eins) + und + zwanzig |
| 22 | zweiundzwanzig | *TSVY-oont-tsvahn-tsikh* | zwei + und + zwanzig |
| 34 | vierunddreißig | *FEER-oont-dry-sikh* | vier + und + dreißig |
| 47 | siebenundvierzig | *ZEE-ben-oont-feer-tsikh* | sieben + und + vierzig |
| 58 | achtundfünfzig | *AHKHT-oont-fuenf-tsikh* | acht + und + fünfzig |
| 99 | neunundneunzig | *NOYN-oont-noyn-tsikh* | neun + und + neunzig |

🔑 **Rule:** `eins` → `ein` when compounded. *einundzwanzig*, never *einsundzwanzig*. [citation:8]

## 7. Telling Time — Formal (Official)

| Time | German | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14:00 | vierzehn Uhr | *FEER-tsayn oor* | 2:00 PM |
| 14:30 | vierzehn Uhr dreißig | *FEER-tsayn oor DRY-sikh* | 2:30 PM |
| 09:15 | neun Uhr fünfzehn | *noyn oor FUENF-tsayn* | 9:15 AM |
| 18:45 | achtzehn Uhr fünfundvierzig | *AHKH-tsayn oor FUENF-oont-feer-tsikh* | 6:45 PM |

💡 Use **Uhr** between hours and minutes. [citation:12]

## 8. ⚠️ Telling Time — Colloquial (Everyday)

**DANGER: `halb` = half TO, not half past.**

| Time | German | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2:30 | halb drei | *hahlp dry* | half **to** 3 = 2:30 |
| 3:15 | Viertel nach drei | *FEER-tel nahkh dry* | quarter past 3 |
| 2:45 | Viertel vor drei | *FEER-tel for dry* | quarter to 3 |
| 3:10 | zehn nach drei | *tsayn nahkh dry* | ten past 3 |
| 2:50 | zehn vor drei | *tsayn for dry* | ten to 3 |

🔑 **Drill until automatic:** *halb drei* = **2:30**, NOT 3:30. [citation:9][citation:15]

## 9. Dates (Datum)

| Element | German | Sounds Like | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Date format | am + ordinal + month | — | am dritten Mai |
| Written | day.month.year | — | 03.05.2025 = 3 May 2025 |
| Year 1995 | neunzehnhundertfünfundneunzig | *NOYN-tsayn-hoon-dert-fuenf-oont-noyn-tsikh* | 19+hundert+95 |
| Year 2002 | zweitausendzwei | *TSVY-tow-zent-tsvy* | 2000+2 |

💡 **Ordinal in dates takes -en:** *am dritten* (on the 3rd), *am zwanzigsten* (on the 20th). [citation:3][citation:10]

## 10. Money & Prices (⚠️ Comma/Decimal SWAPPED)

| English | German | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| €3.50 | 3,50 € | *dry OY-roh FUENF-tsikh* | 3 euros 50 |
| €12.99 | 12,99 € | *tsvoelf OY-roh NOYN-oont-noyn-tsikh* | 12 euros 99 |
| €1,999.00 | 1.999,00 € | *ein-tow-zent-noyn-hoon-dert-noyn-oont-noyn-tsikh OY-roh* | 1,999 euros |

🔑 **German:** comma = decimal, dot = thousands. **English:** dot = decimal, comma = thousands. [citation:6]

## 11. `wie viel` vs `wie viele`

| Phrase | Use With | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **wie viel** | uncountable (singular) | Wie viel Wasser? | How much water? |
| **wie viele** | countable (plural) | Wie viele Äpfel? | How many apples? |

Source: [citation:4]

## 13. ✅ Daily Drill

1. Count **1–20 aloud** twice.
2. Say **10 random numbers 21–99** using unit + und + ten.
3. Write **5 times** in formal and colloquial.
4. Say **3 prices** aloud (remember comma = decimal).
5. Answer: *Es ist halb acht* — what time is it? (**7:30**).
