# 🗣️ German Pronunciation 1 — Vowels

> **Goal:** Distinguish short vs long vowels and pronounce all diphthongs.
> 📝 All example words are **German**. Words marked **⚠️** are loanwords.

---

## 1. Why Vowel Length Matters

German vowels are either **short** or **long** — this changes the word's meaning.

| Word Pair | Sounds Like | Meaning | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stadt / Staat | *shtaht* / *shtaht* | city / state | short vs long **a** |
| ihn / in | *een* / *in* | him / in | long vs short **i** |

💡 **Rule:** Long vowels are often followed by a **single consonant** or an **h**.

## 10. Diphthongs (Double Vowels)

| Diphthong | Word (German) | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| ei = *eye* | Wein | *vine* | wine |
| ei = *eye* | mein | *mine* | my |
| ie = *ee* | Liebe | *LEE-beh* | love |
| ie = *ee* | Spiel | *shpeel* | game |
| eu = *oy* | neu | *noy* | new |
| äu = *oy* | Häuser | *HOY-zer* | houses |
| au = *ow* | Haus | *house* | house |
| au = *ow* | Baum | *boum* | tree |

## 11. Practice List

Read aloud daily, **focusing on length**:

> Stadt — Staat · ihn — in · Bett — Beet
> Mutter — Muter · Kopf — Kopf · Brot — Brot
> Wein — Wien · Liebe — Liebe · neu — Nähe · Haus — Hase

## 12. ✅ Daily Drill

Do this **5 minutes every day** this week:
