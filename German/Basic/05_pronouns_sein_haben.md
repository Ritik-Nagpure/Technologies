# 👤 German Pronouns + sein/haben

> **Goal:** Master the pronoun system and conjugate the two most important German verbs.
> 📝 All example words are German.

---

## 1. Personal Pronouns (Nominative)

| Pronoun | Sounds Like | Meaning | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| ich | *ikh* | I | |
| du | *doo* | you (informal sg.) | friends, family |
| er | *air* | he | |
| sie | *zee* | she | |
| es | *es* | it | |
| wir | *veer* | we | |
| ihr | *eer* | you (informal pl.) | group of friends |
| sie | *zee* | they | |
| Sie | *zee* | you (formal) | always capitalized |

## 2. ⚠️ Three "sie" Forms — How to Tell Apart

| Form | Meaning | How to Tell | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| sie (lowercase) + singular verb | she | verb ends -t | **Sie ist** Lehrerin. |
| sie (lowercase) + plural verb | they | verb ends -en | **Sie sind** hier. |
| Sie (capital S) | you (formal) | always capitalized | **Sie sind** Herr Meyer? |

🔑 **Rule:** Capital **S** = formal you. Lowercase **s** = she or they (context + verb form).

Source: [citation:12]

## 3. The Verb **sein** (to be) — Irregular

| Person | Conjugation | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| ich | bin | *bin* | I am |
| du | bist | *bist* | you are (informal) |
| er/sie/es | ist | *ist* | he/she/it is |
| wir | sind | *zint* | we are |
| ihr | seid | *zite* | you are (pl. informal) |
| sie/Sie | sind | *zint* | they/you (formal) are |

|---|---|---|---|

| du | hast | *hahst* | you have (informal) |
| er/sie/es | hat | *haht* | he/she/it has |
| wir | haben | *HAH-ben* | we have |
| ihr | habt | *hahpt* | you have (pl. informal) |
| sie/Sie | haben | *HAH-ben* | they/you (formal) have |

## 5. Using **sein** — First Sentences

| German | Sounds Like | Meaning | Structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ich bin müde. | *ikh bin MUE-deh* | I am tired. | sein + adjective |
| Du bist nett. | *doo bist net* | You are nice. | sein + adjective |
| Er ist Lehrer. | *air ist LAY-rah* | He is a teacher. | sein + noun (no article) |
| Wir sind aus Indien. | *veer zint ouss IN-dee-en* | We are from India. | sein + aus + place |

💡 **Note:** With professions/nationalities, German **drops the article** — *Er ist Lehrer*, not *Er ist ein Lehrer*.

Source: [citation:2][citation:10]

## 6. Using **haben** — First Sentences

| German | Sounds Like | Meaning | Structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ich habe ein Buch. | *ikh HAH-beh ine bookh* | I have a book. | haben + accusative noun |
| Du hast Zeit. | *doo hahst tsite* | You have time. | haben + noun |
| Er hat Hunger. | *air haht HOONG-er* | He is hungry (lit. has hunger). | haben + noun |
| Wir haben ein Haus. | *veer HAH-ben ine house* | We have a house. | haben + accusative noun |

## 7. Common Expressions with **sein** and **haben**

| German | Sounds Like | Meaning | Verb |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wie alt bist du? | *vee ahlt bist doo* | How old are you? | sein |
| Ich bin zwanzig. | *ikh bin TSVAHN-tsikh* | I am 20. | sein |
| Hast du Geschwister? | *hahst doo geh-SHVIS-ter* | Do you have siblings? | haben |
| Ich habe keine Zeit. | *ikh HAH-beh KINE-uh tsite* | I have no time. | haben |
